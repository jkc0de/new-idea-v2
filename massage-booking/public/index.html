Studio Booking See a demo booking page

Online booking built for massage therapists.

Give clients a simple page to book any therapist, any service, 24/7. You get a calm dashboard for your schedule, staff and clients.

  • Real-time availability, no double bookings
  • Cleanup buffers between sessions
  • Per-therapist hours, services and time off
  • Clients can cancel online within your policy

Your booking page

Share one link on your website, Instagram or Google profile. Clients pick a service, therapist and time in under a minute.

Daily schedule

See every therapist's day side by side. Add walk-ins and phone bookings, mark visits completed or no-show.

Client records
